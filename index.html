      Glossary

      Dose
      One amount of a medicine, taken at one time.
      Strength
      How much active ingredient is in one tablet or one measure.
      Prescriber
      A health worker, such as a doctor, who is allowed to order medicine for you.
      Pharmacist
      A trained expert who prepares and checks medicines.
      Interaction
      A change in how one medicine works when you take it with another medicine.
      mg
      Milligram. One thousandth of a gram.
      h
      Hours. For example, 24 h means 24 hours.
      e.g.
      For example.
      HH:MM
      Hours, then minutes, in 24-hour time. For example, 14:30 is half past two in the afternoon.
      PDF
      Portable Document Format. A file you can print or share.
      ICS
      iCalendar. A file that adds events to a calendar app.
